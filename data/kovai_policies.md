# Kovai Finserv — Customer Policy Handbook

Kovai Finserv Private Limited
Registered office: Avinashi Road, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu 641018
Personal Loans Division — Customer Policy Handbook, Edition 2026.1

This handbook explains the terms that apply to personal loans sanctioned by
Kovai Finserv. It is issued to every borrower at the time of sanction and is
available on request from any branch. Where a scheme-specific exception exists,
the exception is stated inside the relevant section below.

## 1. Foreclosure and Prepayment

You may foreclose (fully prepay and close) your personal loan after you have
paid 12 EMIs. Requests received before 12 EMIs have been paid will be declined.

The foreclosure charge is 2% of the outstanding principal plus GST. The charge
is calculated on the principal outstanding on the date the foreclosure quote is
issued, not on the original sanctioned amount.

EXCEPTION — Kovai Shakti women's scheme: under the "Kovai Shakti" women's
scheme the foreclosure charge is waived entirely after 18 EMIs. Between 12 and
18 EMIs a Kovai Shakti borrower may still foreclose, but the standard charge of
2% of the outstanding principal plus GST applies.

Foreclosure is processed in 7 working days from receipt of the cleared payment.
The No Objection Certificate and the loan closure letter are issued at the end
of that period.

## 2. Refunds

EMI overpayment — if you pay more than the EMI due, or an EMI is collected
twice, the excess is refunded to your registered bank account in 5 working days
from the date the excess is identified or reported.

Processing fees are NOT refundable after disbursement. Once the loan amount has
been credited to your account, the processing fee is treated as fully earned
and no part of it is returned, even if the loan is foreclosed early.

If you cancel the loan before disbursement, the processing fee is 100% refunded
within 10 working days of the cancellation request being accepted.

Refunds are always made to the bank account registered on the loan. We do not
make refunds in cash, to third-party accounts, or by cheque.

## 3. Changing Your EMI Date

You may change your EMI debit date free once per financial year. A second
change in the same financial year costs Rs. 500 plus GST.

Requests must reach us with at least 10 days notice before the next scheduled
EMI date. Requests received with less than 10 days notice take effect from the
following month's EMI instead.

The new date must be between the 1st and the 10th of the month. Dates after the
10th cannot be supported because they fall outside our collection cycle.

A change of EMI date may slightly alter the interest for the transition month,
because interest accrues for the actual number of days between debits. Your
tenure and rate are not otherwise affected.

## 4. Late Payment

If an EMI is not paid on the due date, a late payment penalty of 2% per month
applies on the overdue amount, charged from the day after the due date until
the overdue amount is cleared.

EXCEPTION — first-time defaulters: a 3-day grace period is allowed for
first-time defaulters, once in the loan's lifetime. If the EMI is paid within
those 3 days, no penalty is charged. This concession can be used only once per
loan; every later missed EMI attracts the penalty of 2% per month from the day
after the due date, with no grace period.

Repeated non-payment is reported to the credit bureaus and will affect your
CIBIL score. Bounce charges levied by your bank are separate from the late
payment penalty and are recovered as billed.

## 5. Eligibility

- Age: you must be at least 21 years old at the time of application and not
  more than 58 years old at maturity of the loan.
- Minimum income: Rs. 18,000 per month for salaried applicants and Rs. 25,000
  per month for self-employed applicants.
- Minimum CIBIL score: 680. Applications below 680 are declined.
- Maximum loan amount: Rs. 15,00,000.
- Maximum tenure: 60 months.

Meeting these criteria makes you eligible to apply. It does not guarantee
sanction — every application is also assessed on obligations, repayment
history, and internal credit policy.

## 6. Documents Required

For salaried applicants:

- PAN card
- Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence as proof of identity
- Proof of current residence address
- Latest 3 months salary slips
- Latest 6 months bank statement of the salary account
- Form 16 or the latest income tax return
- One passport-size photograph

For self-employed applicants:

- PAN card
- Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence as proof of identity
- Proof of current residence address
- Proof of business existence — GST registration, Udyam certificate, or
  municipal trade licence
- Latest 2 years income tax returns with computation of income
- Latest 12 months bank statement of the primary current account
- One passport-size photograph

Originals must be produced for verification at the branch. Copies are retained
with the loan file.

## 7. Interest Rates

Interest is charged on a reducing balance basis. Rates range from 10.5% to 18%
per annum, and the rate offered to you depends on your credit score, income,
employment profile and loan tenure.

The rate is FIXED for the full tenure. It does not change with market
movements, repo rate revisions, or changes in our published card rate after
your loan is disbursed.

Kovai does NOT offer floating rate personal loans. If you have been told
otherwise by any agent or intermediary, please report it to our grievance
channel immediately.

## 8. Complaints and Escalation

We handle complaints through 3 escalation levels. Please move to the next level
only after the timeline for the current level has passed.

- Level 1 — Customer Care. Write to care@kovaifinserv.example. We respond
  within 48h.
- Level 2 — Grievance Redressal Officer. If Level 1 has not resolved your
  complaint, write to grievance@kovaifinserv.example. Resolution within
  7 working days.
- Level 3 — RBI Ombudsman. If your complaint is still unresolved, you may
  approach the RBI Ombudsman after 30 days from the date of your original
  complaint.

Always quote your loan account number. Keep the acknowledgement reference from
each level, as the Ombudsman will ask for it.

## 9. Loan Protection Insurance

Loan protection insurance is OPTIONAL. It is never a condition of approval. No
employee or agent of Kovai Finserv may tell you that buying insurance is
required to get your loan sanctioned, disbursed, or approved at a better rate.

If you choose to take it, the premium is 0.35% of the sanctioned amount,
charged once at disbursement. There is no recurring or annual premium.

The cover, exclusions and claim process are governed by the insurer's policy
document, which is issued to you directly by the insurer. Claims are settled by
the insurer, not by Kovai Finserv.
